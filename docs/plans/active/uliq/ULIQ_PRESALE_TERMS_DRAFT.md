## 3. Sale rounds and purchase limits

Round 1 offers up to 50,000,000 ULIQ at 0.002 USDC per ULIQ, with a cap of 100,000 USDC. The minimum purchase is 500 USDC and the cumulative maximum is 10,000 USDC per wallet. Round 2 offers up to 100,000,000 ULIQ at 0.0035 USDC per ULIQ, with a cap of 350,000 USDC. The minimum purchase is 100 USDC and the cumulative maximum is 5,000 USDC per wallet. Limits apply separately to each round. A valid withdrawal restores purchase capacity. There is no minimum fundraising target that automatically triggers a refund. The start and end of each round will be displayed on the presale page before participation.

## 4. Purchase process and custody

Review the active round, accepted USDC amount, ULIQ allocation and wallet transaction before confirming a purchase. An approval alone is not a purchase. A confirmed purchase first creates a pending allocation; it does not immediately deliver transferable ULIQ. Pending USDC remains in the custody contract assigned to that round. At finalization, USDC goes to the seller's designated Safe treasury and the full ULIQ allocation is funded into the round's vesting contract. Use a wallet you control and verify the network.

## 5. Withdrawal and refunds

The contractual withdrawal period is 14 days (1,209,600 seconds) from the recorded purchase time. The buyer may withdraw their own pending purchase through the deadline shown for that purchase. A successful withdrawal cancels the allocation and returns the full recorded USDC amount to the buying wallet. Use the withdrawal action in the purchase history. For access problems or other withdrawal requests, contact [CONTACT EMAIL] promptly with the wallet address and transaction reference; never send private keys or a recovery phrase. After finalization, the contract no longer offers withdrawal of that purchase. Additional rights and remedies required by applicable law remain unaffected.

## 6. Finalization and sale completion

After the withdrawal deadline, any caller may finalize a pending purchase for the original buyer. The caller receives no part of the allocation. A sale pause prevents new purchases but does not remove available withdrawal or matured-finalization paths. Ending a round does not automatically settle all purchases. Both rounds must have no pending purchases and be ready for listing before the shared listing time can be scheduled. Round 2 cannot start until Round 1 has ended. Finalization is separate from the later release of vested tokens.

## 7. Vesting and claims

Both rounds use one shared listing timestamp. Round 1 makes 5% of the finalized allocation claimable at listing. The remaining 95% is released linearly over 548 days after a 90-day cliff. Round 2 makes 25% claimable at listing and releases the remaining 75% linearly over 274 days, without a cliff. No ULIQ is claimable before the shared listing time. Claims are made by the beneficiary wallet; an amount becoming available does not automatically send a transaction. There is no right to accelerate the agreed vesting schedule. Optional locking of wallet-held ULIQ is a separate action and is not performed automatically by the presale.
